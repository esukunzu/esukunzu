memoir & evolution

The Unfiltered Path.

How curiosity turned into craft, and how each small experiment kept becoming a more deliberate way of making.

2020 // the spark

The first click.

I was just curious. I clicked around websites, opened DevTools, and wondered how all of it was built. It felt like a hidden language, and I wanted to learn it.

2021 // tutorial hell

Watching more than building.

I consumed tutorials, bookmarks, and screenshots of polished interfaces. I learned the terms, but not yet the confidence to make something of my own.

2023 // first real code

Something messy, but mine.

I finally started building. The first projects were rough, bug-prone, and unfinished, but each one taught me structure, patience, and the joy of making something real.

2025 // discovery

Design and rhythm.

I began to see that code can be both functional and emotional. Motion, spacing, typography, and timing became part of the story, not just decoration.

“You can’t connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards.”
Steve Jobs
present // the path

Still learning, still building.

I’m not chasing perfection. I’m chasing clarity. I want the work to feel honest, useful, and alive — whether it’s a landing page, a motion study, or an idea that becomes a small digital experience.
