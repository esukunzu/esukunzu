behind the screen

Hello — I'm Esukunzu. I build functional, quiet, and considered spaces for the web.

I care about how things feel when you sit with them. Code is a way to shape attention — timing, type, and space — so a page can stay quiet and still feel alive.

I started from curiosity: how a site holds together under the hood, and why some pages feel calm while others shout. Trial, error, and reading in the open taught more than any single tutorial.

Programming still feels like functional art. I obsess over micro-interactions, typography, and the kind of performance you notice only because nothing gets in the way.
